I am a Master's student in Computational Neuroscience at the **[Bernstein Center for Computational Neuroscience Berlin](https://www.bccn-berlin.de/)**. I currently work as a research assistant in the Neural Data Science and Statistical Computing Group at the **[Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences](https://www.cbs.mpg.de/en)**, privileged to be working under the guidance of **[Nico Scherf](https://www.cbs.mpg.de/person/nscherf/1448721)** alongside an amazing team.

I am interested in biological neural information processing, and I study how both biological brains and artificial neural networks process information. Broadly, my work sits within Computational Neuroscience, with particular interests in [neuroconnectionism](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-023-00705-w) and [NeuroAI](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-37180-x). For a deeper look into my research interest, please check out my[research interests page](/research-interests/) :)

During my Master's, I have worked on several NeuroAI projects. My longest lab rotation focused on autoregressive audio models, supervised by [Greta Tuckute](http://www.tuckute.com/). I am very happy that Greta will also supervise my upcoming Master's thesis. She is a brilliant researcher and a wonderful supervisor. In another lab rotation, I worked on [brain-informed neural networks](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.07.07.662360v2) with [Anuja Negi](http://anujanegi.me/) and [Fatma Deniz](https://www.fatmanet.com/).

Before starting my current work in computational neuroscience, I studied Molecular Biology and Genetics. During my undergraduate studies, I gained valuable research experience at **[TRGENMER](https://uskudar.edu.tr/trgenmer/en)**, where I contributed to gene-therapy projects for rare diseases under the supervision of **[Cihan Tastan](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Cihan-Tastan)**. I also completed a summer internship at the **[Centre for Medical Image Computing (CMIC)](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/medical-image-computing/centre-medical-image-computing-cmic)** at University College London. There, I worked on predictive approaches for Alzheimer's disease using multimodal biomarkers, under the supervision of **[Neil Oxtoby](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/37538-neil-oxtoby)** and **[Maetrei Kohli](http://www0.cs.ucl.ac.uk/people/M.Kohli.html)**.

You can find more information about my academic background and experience in my CV :)

Beyond research, I am passionate about sharing knowledge and building useful tools. I have worked on a range of machine-learning projects, which you can explore on my GitHub. I also strongly believe in maintaining a balance between mental and physical well-being. Outside of research, I enjoy powerlifting, playing the drums, and playing the bağlama—my traditional instrument—as forms of challenge, creative expression, and relaxation.

If you have any questions about my research, projects, or the Master's program, feel free to reach out!
